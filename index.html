01 — What we do

Three honest services, one small shop

A.

New & second-hand machines

Machines from Brother, Bernina, Janome, Husqvarna and Baby Lock — plus the best of the second-hand market. Every used machine is checked and serviced before it goes out, and we'll happily talk you through what actually suits how you sew.

B.

Servicing & repairs

Done on site, not sent away. Timers, tension, feeds, electrics — whatever's wrong, we'll get it sewing again and tell you honestly whether it's worth the fix.

C.

Fabric, haberdashery & craft

Dress and patchwork fabrics, thread, needles, zips, buttons, knitting and crochet yarns, beads, books and gift vouchers — a proper old-fashioned counter of useful things.
