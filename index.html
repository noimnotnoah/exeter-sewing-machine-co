What we do

01 / Services

A

New & second-hand machines

Machines from Brother, Bernina, Janome, Husqvarna and Baby Lock, plus the best of the second-hand market. Every used machine is checked and serviced before it goes out, and we'll talk you through what actually suits how you sew.

B

Servicing & repairs

Done on site, never sent away. Timers, tension, feeds, electrics — whatever's wrong, we'll get it sewing again and tell you honestly whether it's worth the fix.

C

Fabric, haberdashery & craft

Dress and patchwork fabrics, thread, needles, zips, buttons, yarns, beads, books and gift vouchers — a proper old-fashioned counter of useful things.
